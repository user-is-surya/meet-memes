What’s new in

    Memes for your Google Meet camera

    Do a gesture on camera and a meme plays in your video. Everyone in the call sees it, because it’s part of the video you send. It works with Meet’s background blur and virtual backgrounds.

    👀

    What others see

    Your video with the meme in it, and meme sounds mixed into your mic. Nothing is sent while your camera or mic is off in Meet.

    🙈

    What only you see

    The floating 🎭 button, small messages, and the charge ring that fills as a gesture is recognised. None of these are in your video.

    🛑

    Panic button

    Press Alt+Shift+0 any time to turn everything off instantly. Your camera goes back to normal. Press it again to turn things back on.

    Modes

    Only one mode is on at a time. Switch from the 🎭 button on the Meet page, from the toolbar popup, or with the keyboard.

    🎭 Meme modeAlt+Shift+MGestures trigger memes (below).
    ✏️ Draw modeAlt+Shift+DDraw in the air for everyone to see.
    ✨ Spell modeAlt+Shift+SDoctor Strange, Wanda and Doctor Doom style magic.
    ⏻ OffAlt+Shift+0Camera untouched.
    🪞 MirrorAlt+Shift+ROthers see you flipped, like your selfie view. Works in any mode.

    In Meme mode every meme also has its own hotkey (shown on its card), no gesture needed. In Draw mode the same number keys pick ink colours, and in Spell mode they pick the spell style. Alt+Shift+B (bad connection) works in every mode.

    Meme mode

    Each meme has its own gesture. Gestures need to be held clearly for a moment, so ordinary movement in a meeting doesn’t set them off. Watch the charge ring: when it fills, the meme plays.

    ✏️ Draw mode

    Write or sketch in the air with your finger; everyone sees glowing neon ink in your video. Your video is mirrored while you draw, so your writing reads the right way round for everyone (it goes back to your own mirror setting when you leave Draw mode).

    ☝️

    Draw

    Point with your index finger, other fingers folded. The ink follows your fingertip.

    ✌️

    Move without drawing

    Two fingers up (index + middle). A small ring shows where your pen is.

    ✋

    Erase

    Open palm. Wipes the ink under your palm, like a whiteboard eraser.

    🙌

    Clear everything

    Both palms open for one second (watch the charge ring), or Alt+Shift+C.

    🎨

    Colours and brush size

    Along the top of your video is a row of colour dots. Hold your fingertip on one for half a second to pick it. The last dot switches between a thin and a thick brush. Or press Alt+Shift+1…6.

    ↩️

    Undo

    Alt+Shift+Z removes your last stroke. Your drawing stays when you switch modes and comes back when you return to Draw mode.

    Tips: keep your hand inside the picture and a little away from your face. Good light helps the finger tracking. Strokes stay where you drew them, they don’t follow you.

    ✨ Spell mode

    Five hand moves, three styles. Learn the moves once and they work in every style: Strange (orange mandalas and sparks), Wanda (scarlet swirls and mist) or Doom (green runes and crackling lightning). Pick the style with Alt+Shift+1 / 2 / 3 in Spell mode, or in the popup.

    ✋

    Shield

    Hold one open palm up, facing the camera. A spinning magic circle appears on your palm and follows it until you lower your hand.

    🤲

    Orb

    Both open hands in front of your chest, facing each other with a gap between them, like holding a ball. An energy orb glows between your hands; move them apart or together to resize it.

    🫸

    Blast

    Push an open palm quickly toward the camera. A bolt flies at everyone and the screen flashes. Hotkey: Alt+Shift+4.

    ☝️

    Portal

    Draw a circle in the air with your index finger (other fingers folded). A sparking portal opens where you drew it and stays for 6 seconds. Hotkey: Alt+Shift+5.

    🙌

    Channel

    Raise both hands beside your head, fingers spread wide, and hold. Your eyes glow, energy streams from your fingertips and sparks rise around you.

    Held spells (shield, orb, channel) hum while they’re up; blasts and portals have their own sounds. Sounds only go out while your mic is on in Meet.

    Utilities

    🪞

    Mirror my video

    Others see the same flipped view you see in your self-view. Meme text is always drawn the right way round. Text behind you (posters, shirt logos) will look reversed to others; that’s unavoidable. Toggle with Alt+Shift+R, the 🪞 button or the popup.

    📶

    Bad connection

    Fake a choppy, freezing connection when you need an excuse. Press Alt+Shift+B (or 📶 on the floating button, or the popup) to start and again to stop. It starts mild and gets worse over a few seconds: blocky video, stutters, long freezes, and your voice cutting out or going robotic. Pick video only, audio only or both in the popup. Optional: leave the call automatically after 15 s, 30 s or 1 min (you get a 5 second countdown; Alt+Shift+B cancels). It never stays on after you reload or join another call. Your real network is not touched.

    🎙 Help tune the gestures (record a trace)

    A trace is what the gesture detector saw: hand and face positions, no video or sound. Send the files to the developer (or put them in tests/fixtures/) so gestures can be tuned to real hands.

    Troubleshooting

    Others can’t see my memes

    Open the toolbar popup during the call. “Effects path: after Meet effects ✓” means memes go out with your video. If it says fallback, memes still work but Meet’s blur may soften them. Also check that the mode isn’t Off and “Who sees the effects” is set to Everyone.

    Nothing works after installing or updating

    Refresh the Meet tab. The extension has to load before Meet turns on your camera.

    A meme won’t fire

    Use Practice above and watch the meter. Make sure your hands and face are well lit and fully in frame. Hold the pose until the charge ring fills. Each meme’s card explains common mistakes. The hotkey always works as a fallback.

    A meme fires when I don’t want it to

    Turn that meme off in the popup, or switch to Off with Alt+Shift+0. Recording a “normal” trace in Practice helps the developer fix it for good.

    Effects are slow or choppy

    Effects reduce their detail automatically on slower computers. Closing other heavy tabs helps. Your internet speed doesn’t affect the animations, only how sharp your video looks to others.

    Checklist before a big call
    1. Join a test call with a second device.
    2. Fire each meme with its hotkey and check the other device sees it and hears the sound.
    3. Try each gesture once with Practice.
